写作翻译注意

一、语言最重要

最低要求：单词语法正确

  1. 作文中所有不会的单词都换成自己会的单词

  2. 翻译单词不会写 ：

    1. 用我们会的单词把这个单词的意思解释出来
      例：京杭大运河 —— a river from Beijing to Hangzhou
    2. 使用上位词代替
      例：月饼：pie，商业大都市：big city，指南针：a tool
    3. 同义词或近义词

  3. 语法正确 ：长难句替换成简单句

最高要求：

  1. 单词语法多变
    例：认为：think 替换为 believe、argue、claim、contend
    some people think that……other interviewers believe that……I claim that……

二、结构——句子和句子之间逻辑关系词的使用

常见的逻辑关系词（每种背一到两个）

  • 表示原因 ：
    because, in that, as, since
    because of = due to = owing to = thanks to
  • 表示结果 ：
    so = hence = therefore = thereby = thus = consequently = in consequence = as a result/consequence
  • 表示并列 ：
    and = likewise = similarly = equally = simultaneously = at the same time = in the meanwhile = in the same way
  • 表示对比（转折） ：
    but = by contrast = conversely = alternatively = on the contrary = on the other hand = otherwise = while = whereas
  • 表示递进 ：
    besides = also = in addition = moreover = furthermore = apart / aside from this
  • 表示让步 ：
    despite = in spite of = although = though = as = while
    there is no doubt that……（毫无疑问的是……）
    it may be true that……（或许真实的是……）
    there is no denying that……（不可否认的是……）
    in effect/fact/reality = indeed = virtually (事实上)
  • 表示引出新对象 ：
    about = concerning = regarding = with regard to = as for / to = in respect of（关于）
  • 表示总结 ：
    all in all = overall = on the whole = in conclusion / summary

三、内容不重要

内容一点都不重要，只是用来凑字数

句子的分类

结构上分为：

  • 简单句
  • 并列句
  • 复合句

语序上分为：

  • 倒装
  • 正常语序

语态上分为：

  • 主动
  • 被动
